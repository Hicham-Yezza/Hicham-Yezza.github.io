Editorial leadership

Ceasefire Magazine

I founded Ceasefire in 2002 and was its editor-in-chief until 2021. It began in print and moved online in 2010. I set its editorial standards and house style, led an international team of writers, editors and designers, and edited every article it published online, more than 1,300 in all.
