Hicham Yezza.

I lead Responsible AI evaluation at the BBC, drawing on more than twenty years of leadership across technology, media, research and policy.

The thread through my work is evidence, and the judgement needed to act on it. I came to AI evaluation after two decades as an editor, journalist and policy consultant, and I still build the evaluation tools I use. As AI changes how people find and judge information, I'm interested in what organisations owe the public when they adopt it.

Holding AI to editorial standards

I'm lead author of the BBC/EBU News Integrity in AI Assistants Toolkit, which grew out of a study across 22 public-service broadcasters in 14 languages. Before that I helped lead BBC Monitoring's move into data and AI.
