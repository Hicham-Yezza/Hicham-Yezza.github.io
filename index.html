Hicham Yezza

I work on how to evaluate artificial intelligence: whether AI systems are accurate, where they go wrong, and what that means for the people who rely on them.

I'm Responsible AI Evaluation Lead at the BBC and a Visiting Fellow at the University of Reading. I trained as a computer scientist, and bring more than twenty years of leadership across technology, media, research and policy, as an editor, writer and consultant.

Selected work

  • News Integrity in AI Assistants Toolkit

    BBC and EBU, 2025. Lead author.

    Practical guidance on what an accurate, well-sourced AI news answer looks like, with a structured account of the ways AI assistants get news wrong.

  • News Integrity in AI Assistants

    BBC and EBU, 2025. Contributor.

    An international study of AI assistants' answers to news questions, run with 22 public-service media organisations in 18 countries and 14 languages.

  • Small Islands, Genuine Partnerships

    UN DESA and The Partnering Initiative, 2021. Lead author.

    Official UN guidance on building durable partnerships in Small Island Developing States.
