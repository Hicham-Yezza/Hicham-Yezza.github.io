Work

Responsible AI.

My work covers AI evaluation, information integrity, and AI safety and ethics. At the BBC I lead work on evaluating the risks, safety and public value of AI systems, and the results inform decisions about whether, and how, tools are used.

The work

I design evaluation suites and harnesses for tools built on large language models. Most combine expert human review with automated checks, and part of the job is testing whether automated evaluators agree with human reviewers. More of the work is now multilingual.

The work runs in two directions. I turn BBC editorial standards into criteria a system can be tested against, and I turn technical evidence into guidance that editors and senior leaders can act on.

AI and society

Evaluation is also how I approach AI's effects on society. The News Integrity study started from a public question: what happens to trust in news when people get it through AI assistants. I've also spoken about what AI means for skills and work, and about what it takes for an organisation to use AI responsibly.

2025 · Toolkit

News Integrity in AI Assistants Toolkit ↗

Lead author, with James Fletcher and Dorien Verckist (BBC and EBU). It sets out what a good AI news answer needs, covering accuracy, sourcing, quotes, fact and opinion, and context, with a taxonomy of the ways AI assistants get news wrong.

2025 · Study

Contributor. Journalists from 22 public-service media organisations in 18 countries assessed AI assistants' answers to news questions in 14 languages.
